# duffy.jpg

*Capt. Hugh Duffy*, full-length portrait, standing, hands on hips. Photograph by
E. Chickering, Boston, copyright c1902. Library of Congress Prints and
Photographs Division, item 91783093 (https://www.loc.gov/item/91783093/),
reproduction 3c03866, the 818 × 1024 service JPEG.

Published in 1902, so in the public domain in the United States.

Used because it is a real person, facing the camera, head to feet, with the
hands on the hips and the elbows out — the hop pose — against a plain backdrop.
