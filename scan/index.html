BEST · vest sizing

Hop five times. That is the tape measure.

A photograph has no size in it, which is why every sizing app asks how tall you are. This one does not ask anything. While you are in the air you are falling, and everything falls at the same rate — so the camera times you, and gravity tells it how big you are.

No card to hold, no height, no weight. Nothing leaves this device.

  1. Look at the screenfor a second, close up, while it reads your eyes — a cross-check, nothing more.
  2. Stand the tablet upstraight, at chest height. A level on the screen says when.
  3. Step back, hands on hipsuntil you are in the picture from head to feet.
  4. Hop five timessmall hops on the spot, knees straight — from the ankles.
  5. Arms out, then turna picture facing; then arms up and a slow quarter turn, which measures your chest all the way round.

Uses the camera, and the tablet's level if it has one. No microphone. Can't hop? It still works — from your eyes alone, with a wider margin, which the answer will say.
